# Calendar-first semester and member cards

The owner requested Month, Week and List calendar views with a task-only date list, plus a compact member-card directory. The existing warm palette and all save/authorization workflows remain in place.

- Semester opens with the calendar. Its Show control filters events, tasks or both; Tasks only opens the date list. Undated tasks remain explicitly undated and are never placed on today's date automatically.
- Weeks run Monday–Sunday in China time. Timed single-day activities use their entered times; multi-day, date-only and unconfirmed-time items sit in a separate row. Unknown end times stay labeled TBC. Overlapping events use separate lanes.
- Month cells offer an accessible day picker and full day agenda. Small screens use day counts; full titles are available in the selected-day and List views. Editing preserves the selected calendar period/view. Groups, Projects and monthly reviews remain available in secondary controls.
- Member cards show name, email, team position (Leader / Core team / Member), and Active / Inactive. Previous school years, group assignment, contact details and login management are secondary options.
- Directory emails remain behind the existing owner/designated-editor authorization. Position maps to the existing roster metadata, never to Auth account roles. Verified sign-in email bindings are not changed by the card editor.
- Previous support plans no longer has an Admin entry or editor; its former page redirects to current semester Projects. Historical database rows remain intact, and public Plans is unchanged.

Validation covers calendar year/leap/DST boundaries, task filtering and undated records, multi-day ranges, overlapping time layout, and position changes that do not elevate account permissions. Browser interaction review remains constrained by the existing browser access policy.
